01 / Holder recognitionIdentity, not power

Connect.
Be recognised.

Link a Solana wallet to your game account. Alpha can recognise eligible HOWL holdings and make holder-linked cosmetics available — including visual identity items such as frames and skins.

Link wallet→Recognise holdings→Cosmetics

Holder eligibility and available items follow the game's current rules. Your gear, damage and progression are not boosted by owning HOWL.

02 / Treasury VaultOptional cosmetics

Choose a cosmetic.
Support the Pack.

When items and payments are available in-game, players can spend HOWL on eligible cosmetic and supporter items in the Treasury Vault. The tokens are transferred to the project's designated public treasury wallet.

Vault item→HOWL payment→Project wallet

Check the live catalog, payment status and destination inside the game before transacting. Treasury transfers are not token burns or a promise of permanent locking.
