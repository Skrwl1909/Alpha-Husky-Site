01 / Read the record

Proof before
presentation.

The Project Bible is not a feature checklist and not a roadmap dressed up as one. It is a public view of the current Alpha Husky record, based on Alpha Husky Master Bible v1.3 REVIEWED RC2, an evidence-reviewed candidate through 17 October 2026. The manuscript is not final lore approval: disputed Part 5/D11 continuity remains open. Older evidence stays historical. Newer code does not become LIVE until the right evidence exists.
